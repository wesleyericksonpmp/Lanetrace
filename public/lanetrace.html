Lanetrace User Guide

Getting Started

Option 1: Import from Excel

  1. Click "Download template" to get the Excel template
  2. Fill in your data on the "TimelineExcel" sheet
  3. Drag-drop the file into Lanetrace or click "Load .xlsx"

Option 2: Build from scratch

  1. Press N to add a new item
  2. Click any item to edit it
  3. Use Settings (gear icon) to configure swimlanes and colors

Tips
  • Swimlanes are auto-created from unique values in your data
  • Click an item, or Tab to it and press Enter, to edit it
  • Use the Data view tab for spreadsheet-style editing
  • Your edits are auto-saved to browser storage
  • Right-click a row in the Data view to insert or delete rows
